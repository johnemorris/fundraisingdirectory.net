# Final Targeted Provider Ingestion — Batch 4

Date: 2026-09-20

## Remaining Gaps Before Selection

The Batch 4 baseline contains 40 providers, 58 programs, and 136 authoritative sources. All 40 canonical records and the three earlier batch reports were reviewed before selection.

The major donation, crowdfunding, peer-to-peer, event, auction, pledge, restaurant, passive-purchase, grant, product-sale, consulting, and international nonprofit models were already sufficiently represented for V1. The remaining gaps were narrow but useful:

- no `clothing-drive`, `supply-drive`, or `food-drive` activity subtype;
- only one animal/pet cause provider and no pet-specific verified medical crowdfunding option;
- only one print-on-demand provider, leaving little choice within an important low-inventory merchandise model;
- only one gaming-livestream provider and one gaming-integration provider;
- food, donated-goods, supplies, and service outcomes were present but still thin;
- clothing/household reuse and online purchased-goods delivery were absent as distinct collection/in-kind operating models.

The final selection was limited to five providers because each directly closes one of those gaps. No provider was selected merely to increase the corpus count.

## Providers Added

### Savers FUNDrive

- **Program:** Clothing and Household Goods FUNDrive.
- **Fundraising model:** A local, nonprofit-run collection event in which accepted clothing, books, and household goods are delivered and sold to a participating Savers-family store for unrestricted purchase revenue.
- **Exact gap addressed:** Adds the first used-clothing/household-goods fundraiser and a materially different collection/reuse choice beyond shoes and printer cartridges.
- **Important taxonomy:** `collection-reuse-fundraising`, `in-kind-resource-fundraising`, `clothing-drive`, `recycling-drive`, collection logistics, cash, and donated goods.
- **Economics:** Free platform/templates; locally agreed variable purchase payment for accepted goods. The official FAQ says most organizations earn at least $700, but that is preserved as an average rather than a guarantee.
- **Eligibility/requirements:** U.S. participants must be IRS-recognized tax-exempt organizations; Canadian participants must provide applicable government nonprofit recognition. The group signs the agreement, supplies required tax documents and counts, and schedules delivery.
- **Geography:** Local opportunities near participating U.S. and Canadian stores; unavailable in Minnesota, Puget Sound, and Quebec. Accepted goods and terms are store-specific.
- **Authoritative sources (2):** [FUNDrive FAQ](https://fundrive.savers.com/Home/Faq) and [program philosophy/guidelines](https://fundrive.savers.com/Content/PhilosophyAndGuidelines).
- **Unresolved/recheck:** No universal event length, purchase rate, or payment-delivery time is published. These values remain variable rather than inferred.

### YouGiveGoods

- **Program:** Online GoodsDrive.
- **Fundraising model:** Supporters purchase brand-new food, supplies, clothing, hygiene items, books, toys, and similar goods through an online drive; YouGiveGoods delivers the goods directly to the selected charity.
- **Exact gap addressed:** Adds the first structured online `supply-drive` and `food-drive`, plus a direct-to-charity in-kind model without organizer-managed bins or shipping.
- **Important taxonomy:** `in-kind-resource-fundraising`, `supply-drive`, `food-drive`, `clothing-drive`, online store, provider fulfillment, reporting, supplies, food, and donated-goods outcomes.
- **Economics:** No organizer fee or subscription; 100% of purchased goods are delivered to the recipient. Charity partners receiving at least $5,000 in donated goods annually qualify for a documented 5% cash giveback.
- **Eligibility/requirements:** Individuals, companies, schools, groups, and charities may create a drive, choose a U.S. charity, select goods, and share the page.
- **Geography:** Recipient charities and deliveries are within the United States; organizers and supporters participate online.
- **Authoritative sources (4):** [provider overview](https://yougivegoods.com/), [starting a GoodsDrive](https://yougivegoods.com/starting-a-goodsdrive), [individual organizer documentation](https://yougivegoods.com/individuals), and [terms](https://yougivegoods.com/terms-of-use).
- **Unresolved/recheck:** Universal setup, campaign, and delivery timing is not published and is explicitly `researched-unknown`.

### Waggle

- **Program:** Pet Medical Crowdfunding.
- **Fundraising model:** Reviewed pet-medical crowdfunding for guardians and rescues, with direct payment to a verified veterinary provider and possible matching or sponsor support.
- **Exact gap addressed:** Adds the missing animal-specific medical crowdfunding choice and a direct-to-veterinarian safeguards model distinct from general personal crowdfunding.
- **Important taxonomy:** Crowdfunding, peer-to-peer, matching, sponsorship, animal/pet cause, crowdfunding pages, matching gifts, sponsorship management, cash, and services.
- **Economics:** Free campaign creation; a 10% processing fee for rescue campaigns or 15% for pet-guardian campaigns is added at checkout. U.S. physical-check payments to veterinarians may deduct the lesser of $50 or 15%; e-payment has no Waggle payment fee.
- **Eligibility/requirements:** Organizers provide veterinarian contact information, a treatment estimate, and pet photos; Waggle reviews the case and verifies the practice and invoice. Funds do not become unrestricted personal cash.
- **Geography:** United States and Canada; physical checks are limited to U.S. veterinary hospitals.
- **Authoritative sources (6):** [how Waggle works](https://www.waggle.org/about-us/how-we-work), [campaign fees](https://support.waggle.org/hc/en-us/articles/44255793446164-Does-Waggle-take-a-fee-from-donations), [post-campaign process](https://support.waggle.org/hc/en-us/articles/50075583215124-What-Happens-After-My-Pet-s-Campaign-Closes), [BILL payment methods](https://support.waggle.org/hc/en-us/articles/31713562952852-How-does-BILL-work), [veterinary provider requirements](https://support.waggle.org/hc/en-us/articles/360033474531-Does-my-veterinarian-need-to-partner-with-Waggle), and [donation application](https://support.waggle.org/hc/en-us/articles/360033479551-Does-my-full-donation-really-go-to-the-pet-s-care).
- **Unresolved/recheck:** No universal review or campaign duration is published. Payment is typically processed net 15 business days after required veterinary documentation arrives.

### Custom Ink Fundraising

- **Program:** Custom Apparel Fundraising Campaign.
- **Fundraising model:** A hosted custom-apparel sales and donation campaign with print-on-demand production, direct or bulk delivery, and beneficiary disbursement.
- **Exact gap addressed:** Adds a second materially useful print-on-demand option with product-specific print minimums, group/direct/hybrid delivery, and organizer/third-party/charity beneficiary paths.
- **Important taxonomy:** Product sales, donations, crowdfunding, apparel/spirit wear, merchandise store, print-on-demand, online store, fulfillment, and crowdfunding pages.
- **Economics:** No upfront cost; variable profit based on sale price minus production, platform, design, and delivery costs; 2.9% processing on additional or donation-only contributions. Merchandise proceeds require the campaign to meet its displayed print minimum.
- **Eligibility/requirements:** Organizers may be individuals or organizations. Users aged 13–17 need adult consent and supervision; verified charity beneficiaries must be eligible U.S. 501(c)(3) organizations.
- **Geography:** U.S. nationwide with shipping to U.S. territories/APO addresses and a published list of select international destinations; international fees, duties, and turn times vary.
- **Authoritative sources (6):** [how it works](https://www.customink.com/fundraising/how-it-works), [platform economics](https://www.customink.com/help_center/fundraising-how-does-custom-ink-fundraising-make-money), [cost and print minimum](https://www.customink.com/help_center/fundraising-is-custom-ink-fundraising-free-to-use), [disbursement timing](https://www.customink.com/help_center/fundraising-how-and-when-will-the-funds-be-sent), [shipping countries](https://www.customink.com/help_center/fundraising-where-does-custom-ink-fundraising-ship), and [terms](https://www.customink.com/fundraising/user-agreement-policies).
- **Unresolved/recheck:** Product economics and print minimums are campaign-specific. The organizer chooses the campaign duration; no universal duration is asserted.

### Streamlabs Charity

- **Program:** Charity Livestream Fundraising.
- **Fundraising model:** Approved charities maintain profiles while gaming and creator streamers publish dated fundraising campaigns with donation pages, alerts, QR codes, goals, rewards, and reporting.
- **Exact gap addressed:** Adds a second gaming/creator livestream fundraising option and a 0%-platform-fee alternative to the sole existing livestream provider.
- **Important taxonomy:** Donations, peer-to-peer, events, gaming-livestream, creator-livestream, livestream and gaming integrations, QR giving, donation pages, and reporting.
- **Economics:** No account or annual charge and 0% Streamlabs platform fee; third-party processor fees vary by payment method, currency, charity, and donor.
- **Eligibility/requirements:** Charities supply organization/tax information and pass review, usually in one to three business days. Fundraisers verify an account, choose an available charity, and set campaign dates and a goal. U.S. donations are restricted to eligible 501(c)(3) organizations under current compliance rules.
- **Geography:** International reach is documented through global creator platforms, government tax IDs, and international currencies, but no single definitive charity-country list is published.
- **Authoritative sources (5):** [charity profile setup](https://support.streamlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260800840970-How-to-Create-a-Streamlabs-Charity-Profile), [campaign setup](https://support.streamlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260800841090-How-to-Start-a-Charity-Fundraising-Campaign), [livestream fundraising](https://support.streamlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413170327067-How-to-Start-a-Live-Stream-for-Charity), [charity reporting](https://support.streamlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260800791989-How-to-Export-Data-as-a-Charity), and [U.S. compliance update](https://support.streamlabs.com/hc/en-us/articles/40564944768539-Important-Update-for-Streamlabs-Charity-Users-Changes-in-Light-of-California-Assembly-Bill-488).
- **Unresolved/recheck:** The universal supported-country list and processor settlement timing are unavailable; both remain explicit rather than inferred.

## Final Coverage Assessment

### Sufficiently represented for V1

- direct donations, crowdfunding, and peer-to-peer fundraising;
- events, auctions, pledge/a-thons, challenges, and livestream fundraising;
- school/team, nonprofit, faith, community, animal-rescue, and individual/family audiences;
- ordinary product sales, print-on-demand apparel, prepared foods, treats, coffee, flowers, gifts, coupon/savings, gift cards, and purchase-linked rewards;
- restaurant/business partnerships, sponsorships, giving matching, workplace giving, grant research/management, and fundraising consulting;
- collection/reuse through shoes, cartridges/electronics, vehicles, and clothing/household goods;
- in-kind support through care coordination and purchased food/supply/goods drives;
- U.S. nationwide, local, Canadian, and international operating models;
- fixed, percentage, tiered, variable, no-fee, subscription, revenue-share, product-margin, give-back, and direct-to-provider economics.

### Represented but worth deepening later

- enterprise workplace giving and CSR administration;
- gaming and creator fundraising integrations beyond the two current options;
- specialized veteran, hunger, animal, transplant, mental-health, and arts cause discovery;
- print-on-demand and textile-reuse alternatives;
- provider enrichment where current pricing, payout, or geographic documentation is intentionally variable.

These are future depth/enrichment opportunities, not missing V1 fundraising choices.

### Intentionally deferred

- generic payment processors and ecommerce tools that are not fundraising providers;
- beneficiary-specific charities that do not provide a reusable fundraising service;
- poorly documented or private/quote-only enterprise products that cannot support a defensible comparable profile;
- exhaustive duplication of every product vendor and every taxonomy value.

### Dependent on future location/ZIP functionality

- Savers FUNDrive store participation, accepted goods, and local purchase terms;
- restaurant appointment inventory and store-specific economics;
- regional matching-grant and local opportunity availability;
- location-level pickup, delivery, and redemption details.

Provider records accurately state these boundaries but do not imitate the future location subsystem.

## Schema Stress

No important fact was lost, made misleading, forced into the wrong concept, or attached to the wrong provider/program.

- **Unsupported payout cadences in Batch 4:** 0. None of the five selected providers added a recurring payout frequency outside the frozen enum.
- **Accumulated cadence pressure:** Earlier batches still contain daily, weekly, twice-weekly, biweekly, and semiannual/anchored-twice-yearly schedules preserved in timing notes. The evidence is repeated and cross-provider, but remains nonblocking because the exact facts are retained separately from elapsed payout duration.
- **Local program variability:** Savers' store-specific opportunity, accepted-goods, and purchase terms fit accurately in provider geography and program notes, but need future location entities for discovery-level availability.
- **Goods rather than cash:** YouGiveGoods combines free organizer access, purchased-goods delivery, and a conditional annual charity giveback. Program-specific economics plus food/supplies/donated-goods outcomes preserve the distinction without pretending the charity receives the donor's purchase as unrestricted cash.
- **Restricted direct payment:** Waggle's campaign funds go to a verified veterinary provider and are capped by the invoice. Requirements, logistics, and services outcome preserve that safeguard; the organizer is not presented as receiving unrestricted cash.
- **Variable print minimum:** Custom Ink's product-specific minimum and resulting profit cannot be reduced to one number. The schema preserves both as variable values while keeping delivery-method timing in notes.
- **Zero platform fee versus processor fees:** Streamlabs' 0% platform fee and variable third-party processing are separate arrangements, preventing a misleading claim that all payment handling is cost-free.

There is no true schema blocker. The schema and Finder ranking policy were not changed.

## Post-Ingestion Cleanup

The following small, evidence-backed cleanup tasks are justified after ingestion but were intentionally not implemented in this batch:

1. Review a controlled `payout_schedules.frequency` expansion for `daily`, `weekly`, `twice-weekly`, `biweekly`, and `semiannual`, with validation and migration tests. Preserve `anchor` for fixed-date schedules.
2. Review the payout delivery-method vocabulary for repeatedly documented methods such as eCheck, PayPal, wire, virtual card, and provider-specific international transfer services. Do not generalize it into a broad payment framework.
3. Recheck the Little Caesars conflicting official `$6`/`$7` claim on the normal editorial schedule; keep `needs-recheck` until authoritative sources converge.
4. Plan location/ZIP entities separately for store, restaurant, regional-grant, pickup, and local-availability discovery. Do not retrofit those entities into provider records.
